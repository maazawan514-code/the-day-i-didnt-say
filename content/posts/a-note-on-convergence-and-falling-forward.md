There is a certain shame attached to trajectories that are not smooth. Society tends to reward clean curves, stable growth, and forward motion that looks like an arrow.

But if you study sequences in calculus, you discover that convergence does not require monotonic movement. A sequence can overshoot, backtrack, and still approach its limit.

I like to think of personal progress in the same way. Sometimes we fall forward. Sometimes we pause. Sometimes we change direction. And yet the whole can still be moving toward something meaningful.
