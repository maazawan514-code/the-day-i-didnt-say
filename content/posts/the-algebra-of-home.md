The algebra of home is found in small rituals:

- the single spoon left in the coffee jar,
- the way the keys always seem to end up on the same table,
- the map of where the shoes are dropped when someone arrives.

The variables are subtle:
How long will the kettle take to boil on a winter morning?
Which song will be humming in the kitchen?
Who will need the umbrella today?

There is a constant, though, too: the willingness to make room for unscheduled changes.
