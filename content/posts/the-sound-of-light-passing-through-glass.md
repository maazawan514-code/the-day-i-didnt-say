The pane of glass becomes a thin membrane
between two densities of air.

Rain writes faint equations across the sheet.
Each droplet slows the light and bends it,
folding brightness into subtle islands on the floor.

Listen for the silence between the drops.
It is where the light passes through,
not making a sound but changing everything.
