The first equation I ever loved was simple:

$$2x + 3 = 7$$

It was not pretty, but it felt alive.

When I isolated $x$ and found that $x = 2$, the page seemed to have rearranged itself to make room for a hidden answer.

It was the first time I understood that symbols were not just marks. They were invitations to discover structure.

Later, I would learn that the real magic was not in the answer but in the process of finding it.
